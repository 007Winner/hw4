# Campus Customs design

The storefront now uses an Oxford-inspired visual system: ink navy, warm archival paper, oxblood, and antique copper; Playfair Display headlines; generous editorial spacing; and a small seal-like brand mark. Product cards gain staggered entrance motion, image zoom, lift states, and clear price/stock hierarchy. The hero adds a quiet collegiate seal animation and layered ring detail, while the chat uses a compact concierge panel with a copper rule, responsive states, and lifted recommendation cards.

These details make the catalogue feel considered and collectible rather than transactional. Strong hierarchy helps shoppers scan quickly, tactile hover states invite exploration, and restrained motion gives the site polish without distracting from products or accessibility.
